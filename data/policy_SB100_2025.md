# Harbor Ridge Bank - Small Business Loan Policy SB-100
Version 2025.1 | Effective: 2025-01-01

## 1. Hard eligibility (all required)
- Business operating at least 18 months at the date of application.
- Loan amount between $25,000 and $250,000.
- Unsecured loans are capped at $150,000.
- Excluded industries: cannabis, gambling, adult entertainment.
- Maximum term: 84 months.

## 2. Credit and cash-flow tests
- Owner credit score: 660 or higher to qualify. 620-659 = refer. Below 620 = decline review.
- DSCR 1.20 or higher = meets test. 1.10 to below 1.20 = refer. Below 1.10 = decline review.

## 3. Required documents
Two years of tax returns, six months of bank statements, photo ID, business license, signed application.

## 5. Pricing
700+ = 8.75%; 680-699 = 9.75%; 660-679 = 11.25%. Origination fee 1.0%.
